About Me

I am a Software Engineer III at Google London, currently pivoting full-time into Technical AI Safety and Alignment Engineering. My core mission is to help build and deploy verifiable, aligned frontier systems.

Having completed BlueDot Impact’s Technical AI Safety curriculum, I am currently deep in the ARENA program, developing practical interpretability tools with PyTorch and NumPy. I am preparing for a fellowship in the Spring 2027 SPAR cohort.

Mechanistic Analysis & Systems Resilience

I view alignment through a combined lens of mechanistic analysis and systems resilience: as AI systems gain tool access and agency, preventing failure modes like deceptive scheming or sandbox escapes requires both looking inside the weights (circuit-level interpretability) and hardening the environment outside them (runtime isolation and secure execution).

Love to connect with people on the same boat.
