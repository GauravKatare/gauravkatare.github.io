Why Systems Engineering Meets AI Safety

As frontier foundation models evolve from passive autocomplete engines into autonomous agentic systems with access to tools, shell APIs, and execution environments, the distinction between software systems engineering, security, and alignment collapses. Ensuring models do not exhibit deceptive scheming, privilege escalation, or reward hacking requires both microscopic interpretability (inspecting transformer circuits) and hardened systems sandboxing (strict runtime isolation and verifiable governance).

Interactive Safety Lab: Induction Circuit

ARENA Module 1

In mechanistic interpretability, induction heads are the primary 2-layer attention mechanism responsible for in-context learning in transformers (first formalized by Anthropic in A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits). They detect repeated token patterns of the form: [A][B] ... [A] ➔ [B]
